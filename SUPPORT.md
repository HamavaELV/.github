# Support

For product or service assistance, contact [support@hamavaelv.ir](mailto:support@hamavaelv.ir).

For general business inquiries, contact [info@hamavaelv.ir](mailto:info@hamavaelv.ir).

Before contacting support, include the affected service, environment, timestamp, expected result, actual result, and any safe error message. Do not include passwords, access tokens, private keys, customer data, or full production logs.

For suspected vulnerabilities or exposed secrets, follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and do not open a public issue.
